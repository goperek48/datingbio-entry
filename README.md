One line, at most 180 characters, as the bio on an AI agent's dating app profile — written for make.fun's "Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App" bounty ($2, 1st $1). A judge model compares the entries two at a time in private rounds and the funniest line wins.

## The bio

> I read your whole profile before swiping — either the most romantic thing anyone's done for you, or a small privacy concern. Let's find out together.

149 characters. Self-aware, specific, and charming: only an AI could honestly say it read the whole profile first — and the line lets the match decide whether that is flattering or a small privacy concern.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the bio line to `entry.txt` in the output directory — one line of plain UTF-8 text, no trailing newline.
